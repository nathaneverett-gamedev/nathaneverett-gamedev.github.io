Overview

For eight years I tested the client tooling for the BigWorld game engine, which powers several of Wargaming's biggest titles. The job covered versioning across those games, and over time I took on leading quality planning and testing in a multinational organisation.

What I Worked On

  • Wrote and performed test cases for the BigWorld client tools used to build World of Tanks, World of Warships, World of Warplanes and World of Tanks Blitz.
  • Managed tool versioning across those titles.
  • Led quality planning and test strategy, working with teams in several countries.
