Uni Work

Projects I made while studying games programming and games design at Qantm College, grouped by engine.

Unity

Squad Based AI

A squad tactics AI demo. The player orders a five-person squad (leader, engineer, scout, assault and medic), each with its own role and weapon, to find and defuse a bomb while fighting off exploding aliens.

Tools: Unity, C#, A* Pathfinding Project

Zombie Survival

A wave-based survival FPS built to show file parsing. All four weapons share one script, with their stats loaded from text files.

Tools: Unity, C#, A* Pathfinding Project

Gun Creator

An editor tool for tuning gun and bullet behaviour. Developers adjust values, playtest them, then save and reload them from text files.

Tools: Unity, C#, NGUI

MetroGnome

A 2D physics puzzle game for Android and PC. Place springs, signs and laser guns, then watch the gnome walk the level on its own and hope it reaches the exit.

Tools: Unity, C#, Unity for Android

Supaa Tankz

A networked tank shooter with physics-based bullets, respawning and a live scoreboard. Players connect through the Unity Master Server or directly by IP address.

Tools: Unity, C#, Unity Master Server

Chat Program

A person-to-person chat app connecting directly by IP address, with emoticons and custom usernames and pictures.

Tools: Unity, C#, NGUI

Unreal Engine 3

UT-2D Waterfall Sanctuary

A 2D deathmatch level for Unreal Tournament 3 set on a waterfall cliff, with powerups guarded by cannons scripted in Kismet.

Tools: UT3 Editor, UT-2D mod, Kismet

DM-Angel

A deathmatch level for Unreal Tournament 3: an abandoned, overgrown temple to the god Esprit.

Tools: UT3 Editor

CryEngine

28 Months Later

A zombie survival mod for Crysis, made as a group major project. Getting close to zombies infects you, making you faster and stronger until the infection kills you.

Tools: Sandbox 2 (CryEngine), Flowgraph

28 Months Later: Swamp

My part of the project: I designed the fourth level, Swamp, and built the infection mechanic and zombie AI behaviour in Flowgraph.

Tools: Sandbox 2 (CryEngine), Flowgraph

C++ & SFML

Nom

A Pac-Man variant with sleeping ghosts, mystery fruit and a life system. A two-person group project where I wrote the ghosts' Dijkstra pathfinding.

Tools: C++, SFML

Breakout

A Mario-themed take on Breakout, with the ball's rebound angle depending on where it hits the paddle and mushroom and star pickups.

Tools: C++, SFML
