Current Prototypes

Board and card games I'm currently designing, in various stages of development. Click a game to find out more.

These are prototypes. Any artwork shown is placeholder art that isn't my own; these games are being pitched to publishers, who will commission the final artwork before release.

Games Currently Pitching

Pitching Scribe

The Pharaoh requires a new Royal Bureaucrat! Write hieroglyphs onto your sacred text. Carefully balance your grid to fulfil objectives and positional scoring, while performing your unique ritual puzzle.

Games You Can Play

Play Online Hieroglyphs

Tableau builder meets spatial puzzle. Position and manipulate dual-purpose papyrus cards on your board for immediate rewards, and complete rows of hieroglyphs to gain the favour of the Gods.

Play Online Garden Warfare

The garden is brimming for war. A grid battler where you place critters in a 3x3 grid to outscore the opponent critter opposite and gain win tokens.

Play Online Don't Get Splat

A paintball drafting game. Draft cards, build your colour stacks, and try to end each round with the lowest score.

Mid Development

Encroach: Monster Siege

Monsters are rising across the world and the cities are next. A co-operative boss battler for 1–4 players where time is your most precious resource. Spend mana to cast spells and summon creatures through the mana vortex, but reinforcements take turns to arrive, and the boss is counting down too. Hold the walls, buy time and bring the monster down before the city falls.
