Objectives

In the Objective phase, select a completed line on your board, then claim an available objective.

Gods

In the Manipulate phase, discard one selected hand card to invoke a God. Isis gives glyph tokens; Bastet moves one card into an empty space and grants an Oasis; Tawaret swaps a grid card with a market card, then draws a card and gains a Refresh token.
