🌳 The Veiled Forest

roll & grow prototype
Design assumptions in this build — read before play-testing
  • Shared die, parallel actions (roll-and-write style): each of the 4 dice actions per round has one "picker" (rotates through the seated players) who reveals a die by naming a colour or a number. Once it's revealed, every player independently chooses to take the Colour action, the Number action, or Pass with that same die. Finishing that core action doesn't commit it right away — it shows a Finalize Turn button instead, a deliberate window to use any free action first (place or move a Sprout, Bulb bonuses, Treetop/Row/Guild claims) before locking your vote in. The round advances once every seated player has clicked Finalize Turn.
  • Colour action, two explicit steps: the wheel is two independently-printed rings (outer + inner) sitting on top of each other. At the start of every round the inner ring is spun 1–4 notches by a die roll (shown under the wheel and animated into place) — this reshuffles which colour pairs with which for that whole round. Step 1, Collect Resources — +2 of the die's own colour is automatic, then the player picks the second resource from wherever that colour currently sits on either ring: its outer-ring slice pairs with one colour, its inner-ring slice with a different one — whichever colour ends up lined up with itself on both rings that round just doubles straight to +4 of its own resource, no second-resource choice. Step 2, Climb the Oak — its own button, shown only once Step 1 is locked in: advances that colour's Oak by 1 rung and fires that rung's reward. Nothing in either step is automatic or bundled into the other.
  • Oaks top out at rung 6 (rung 0 is your starting cube, below rung 1 — shown as coloured cube tokens, your random colour for the game with your letter printed on it, in the Dice Bag's mini towers and on the full boards under The Oaks tab). Every climb moves a single rung; once an Oak is already at 6, that climb banks 1 Point (the red star) instead of going higher. An Oak Climb card only ever prints the plain triangle icon ("move, don't activate") on its own ability, same as Black's "mulch, no activation" bonuses — so climbing via an Oak Climb card (Main, Trade or Wild) never fires the target rung's own reward (resource/sprout/discard/etc), it's pure cube movement (plus the card's own bonus Points, if it's the paid kind) — but the Tag at rung 2/4/6 still comes with it, since that reflects the cube's position, not "activating" something. A Colour die climb, or a treetop/row bonus that explicitly says "full reward", fires the rung's full reward same as always.
  • Number action: Grow or Mulch up to 2 cards from your own hand into the tree matching that number (each card's own printed die faces say which tree numbers it's allowed into; some also need a minimum Oak level to grow, shown in red on the card — mulching is never gated). Grow costs the card's own printed Water+Sunlight and slides it to the top; Mulch costs the Dirt printed on the card's brown strip (1/3/5 for resource cards, 2/4 for Point cards) and tucks it at the bottom; each activation of that Base then yields the strip's printed amount (1/2/3 resources, or 1/2 Points). Then — whether or not you added anything — that tree offers to activate, bottom to top: the Base-collect step always comes first (collects anything mulched there, plus its own printed reward regardless — same as every other "activate a Base" source), then every grown card's ability, each presented one at a time as its own Activate / Skip choice — nothing fires automatically.
  • Base cards print their own activation reward too (bottom-centre icon on the base card art, below the 6-Dirt upgrade row) — transcribed per letter set and tree number: two fixed resources (e.g. A1 → 1 Water + 1 Card), or a wild icon letting you pick any one resource, including a Card (A4, B2, C2, D1). It's granted every time that base activates, on top of whatever it collects from mulched cards in that tree. The same "wild, your choice, Card included" set of options also shows up for the Trade Points wildcard reward.
  • Bulb bonus actions spend your own Bulbs, purely local to you — they never touch the shared die or anyone else's turn. Two are tied to whichever action you've just chosen, and only appear inside that action's own panel: once you Take Colour, 3 Bulb lets you re-pick which colour that action resolves as (as many times as you can afford, until you click Collect Resources — once resources are banked, the colour is locked for the Climb-the-Oak step too); once you Take Number, 1 Bulb nudges which tree it targets by 1 (wrapping 1↔4), but only before you've added a card — the whole action commits to one tree, same as Colour commits to one colour. You can't split the benefit (e.g. collect as Red, then switch to Yellow to climb that Oak instead) — adjust first, then lock it in. The other two are standalone, in the "Sprout bonus actions" panel, usable any time in the dice-action phase (not during round-end sprout activation): 2 Bulb relocates a Sprout you've already placed to a different eligible spot without passing back through supply; 4 Bulb gains a Sprout straight to supply.
  • Sprouts are a second, separate way to activate, one card at a time, outside of the Number action. You start with 1 in your general supply. As a free action, anytime, place one on any base card (circle button, top-right) — it will offer to collect only that tree's mulched resources plus its own printed reward — or on any sprout-eligible grown card, which alone will be offered (not the rest of the tree). Place and un-place freely while supply allows. At round end, switch to each player's tab and hit "Activate Sprouted Cards" — pick WHICH placed Sprout to activate next (your choice of order, same picker Activate Sprout/the Oaks use), each one its own Activate/Skip, repeating until none are left or you stop. A placed Sprout is a standing marker, not a one-shot token — activating (or skipping) it never un-places it or returns it to supply; it stays right there, ready for the next "activate a Sprout" opportunity, whether that's next round's round-end step, an Activate Sprout card, or an Oak-climb bonus. Only the free un-place toggle or the 2-Bulb move action actually touch supply/placement.
  • Grow/Mulch "grow or mulch a second card" bonuses pay full price (the card's own printed Water+Sunlight, or its printed mulch Dirt) — nothing is free unless the card's own ability says "discount," in which case you pay the printed 1-resource cost first and the discount applies on top. Either way the second card can go into any of its own tree numbers, not just the tree whose activation triggered it — pick the card, then pick which of its trees.
  • Card abilities, transcribed from the real art in cards/export (not approximated; 104 cards, named by action): Grow / Grow (Discount) → grow a second card (full price, or pay 1 resource for the printed Water/Sun discount); Mulch / Mulch (Discount) → mulch a second card (full price then activate the Base, or pay 1 resource for -1 Dirt); Activate Sprout → activate one of your placed Sprouts; Double Tag → gain 1 Point (plus 2 different wild Tags when grown); Big Point → 3 Points + a resource; Oak Climb → +1 devotion to its printed Oak (Main: free; Trade: pay 2 of a resource, +2 Points; Wild: pay any 2, your choice of Oak); Activate Discard → discard a hand card to activate that card's own ability, in full — not just its simple/free effects: a paid or choice-needing ability (Oak Climb Trade, Gain Sprout, Grow, …) opens its usual payment/choice step right there, same as a normal activation; Gain Sprout / Gain Tag → pay the printed resource pair (sometimes including discarding a card) for a Sprout / any Tag; Collect Wheel → pay 2 of the printed resource (any 2 for the Wild cards) to collect a wheel sector (fixed target, or your choice for the Wild cards); Trade 2/3/5 Points, Trade 3 Points (Wild), Trade Res + Point → pay the printed cost for Points, sometimes plus 3 of a bonus resource; Trade Wild Res → pay 1 Point for any 3 resources of your choice. A few fine details — exactly which grown cards show the "sprout-eligible" icon, and the precise wording of a couple of ambiguous reads — are flagged in the extraction notes and may need a touch-up.
  • Critters & Leaves (setup screen toggle, off by default; art from cards/CritterLeaves/export, 28 cards): split left/right cards added as one of a Number action's 2 cards, into a tree matching their dice — never grown or mulched. Bloom a Leaf (Water + Dirt) beside a grown card in that tree, 1 per side: on the left it's the limited half (2 uses, tracked with cubes, then it stays, spent), on the right the unlimited half. A Leaf fires straight after its card, every time that card activates in the tableau — its own Activate/Skip; with 2, the player picks which goes first. Entice a Critter (Bulb + Sun) onto that tree's Base, 1 per side: left = ongoing half, right = end-game half. Fox (Thrifty): growing in this tree costs 1 less Water or Sun (taken off whichever you're shorter on), mulching 1 less Dirt / 1★ per grown or mulched card in this tree. Fox (Tags): each Tag gained in this tree (grown cards' printed Tags, the Base upgrade) may bring any 3 resources, taken from the Fox panel / 2★ per Tag in this tree. Leaves' printed stars score at game end; neither type counts toward the grow cap, Guild goals or rows.
  • Test cards (setup screen toggle, off by default; art from cards/Test/export, 47 cards): Activate Column → activate another grown card in this tree, or this tree's Base; Activate Row → activate the grown card in the same grown position of another tree (neither can be discarded to activate; a Row/Column chain can activate each card or Base only once, so a Row may trigger a Column that picks yet another card); Activate Oak Level 2 → pay its printed resource to fire a fixed Oak's rung-2 reward (Wild: pay any 2, any Oak); Big Scoring → any 1 resource; Big Scoring (Oak Top) → 2 Points; Discard for Tag → discard a card for its printed Tag (max 1); Trade 6 Points → 4 of a resource for 6 Points; Trade Res for Wild → 3 of a resource for any 3 others; Trade Sprout → a Sprout (from supply or any placed one) for 5 Points. A resource icon with no number means 1.
  • Tags (5, matching the wheel colours — real icons from Tags/) come from two sources: growing a card that prints one (granted instantly, not at activation — a few cards print the wild-tag icon instead of a fixed colour and let the player choose which Tag(s) to gain) and reaching rung 2, 4, or 6 on an Oak (from any climb source — Colour die, its neighbour pick, or a card activation; see the Oaks note above). Every grant — from any source — now also feeds a running per-colour count: whenever it crosses a fresh multiple of 3 (3, 6, 9…), that's banked as a Treetop bonus claim, shown in a standing "Bonus actions" panel on the player's own tab. Claiming is a genuinely free-standing bonus action, usable at any time in any phase (including mid round-end Sprout activation) — it's never an interrupt, just an always-available option sitting in that list until spent. Picking which of that colour's 5 printed options to claim only needs a free moment (the button disables while you're mid-resolving something else); all 5 Oaks' treetop options are now implemented.
  • Row bonus (the second free-standing bonus action, same "Bonus actions" panel): there are exactly 5 possible rows, each uniform — never a mix of types across trees — and each a one-time trigger: 1 Grown card in every tree, 2 Grown cards in every tree, 1 Mulched card in every tree, 2 Mulched cards in every tree, and every Base upgraded. Completing any of the 5 banks a claim: go up and activate any one Oak of your choice — full reward, your pick of colour, same "claim whenever you like" pattern as Treetop bonuses.
  • Scoring, two kinds: the ⭐ red star is current points, banked as Tag/Trade-Points cards activate and various Oak/treetop bonuses resolve. The 🟣 purple star is each card's fixed end-game value — it only shows on a card's grown (black, full-ability) side, so the live total in your resbar (and your final score) only ever counts grown cards; a mulched card contributes nothing here even though it's still sitting in the tree. Final score (tallied in the Game Complete banner, players sorted highest to lowest) = ⭐ Points gained through the game + (leftover resources & cards in hand, summed, ÷4 rounded down) + 🟣 purple stars on grown cards + 🟣 purple stars on upgraded Bases (a flat 5 each, confirmed fixed across every letter set and tree number on the real cards) + 🟣 purple stars for the rung level reached on all 5 Oaks (the same 2/3/4/5/6/8 track printed on every board, rungs 1-6) + your 2 chosen Guild cards' own scored Points.
  • Grove display: grown cards in a tree show only their black (ability) half; tucked cards show only their brown (mulch) strip — both crop the same real card image, so tap one to see it in full. Hand cards still show the whole card since you're choosing from them.
  • Hand of cards: everyone starts with 5 real cards dealt from the full 104-card deck (see "My Hand" in your Grove). The White wheel resource now literally means "draw 2 cards into hand" instead of a plain counter.
  • Starting resources: a 5-card hand, 1 Sprout, plus 3 each of Water, Sunlight, Dirt, Bulb. Each player's lettered base-card set (A-D) is now randomly assigned (shuffled), not simply dealt in seat order — Player A isn't always seat 1.
  • Solo-game auto-turns: with only 1 player seated, there's no second player to pass the picker role to, so the 2nd and 4th dice action of every round reveal a fully random colour and number instead of letting you choose either — simulating an opponent's turn and pushing you to lean on Bulb die-adjustment (change the number/colour locally) to cope with whatever comes up. The 1st and 3rd actions each round are still yours to pick normally.
  • Guild cards (real art in Guilds/, now assets/guilds/): before the game proper begins, every player is dealt 2 "A" and 2 "B" cards (shared shuffled decks, so no repeats across the table) and keeps 1 of each — this is the Choose your Guild cards screen shown first. Each scores bonus Points at game end off its own tiered threshold (e.g. "8 of these → 6pts, 10 → 12pts, 12 → 20pts" — the highest tier reached, not cumulative). All 10 A's are the same shape (total Tags across 2 named colours); all 12 B's are now implemented too, including the ones needing new bookkeeping to score correctly: every Tag now records whether it came from an Oak climb or a grown card's own fixed (non-wild) printed Tag — wild-sourced Tags (choose1/choose2 cards, wild-tag bonuses, Trade/Discard-tag cards) record neither, per "wild doesn't count"; and every Oak/treetop bonus that actually fires a Point gain/cost, an Activate-a-Sprout, an Activate-a-Mulch, or a Mulch-only-discount effect now ticks a running per-player usage counter for that icon, regardless of what triggered the climb (die, card, or a re-activation bonus).
  • Base card upgrade (pay 6 Dirt to flip) counts as a mulch-category action, never a free standalone one — it's offered as an alternative everywhere the game already offers "mulch a card": the Number action's own 2 "add" slots, and any Oak/treetop/card bonus that mulches (Forge, discount/no-activation variants, mulch-multi). Picking it instead of a card pays the same 6 Dirt and, for the full-price "mulch & activate" sources, still triggers that Base's own activation afterward. Upgrading also grants a wild Tag immediately (your choice of colour, same picker as a wild-tag card) and is worth 5 purple end-game stars at scoring (see UPGRADED_BASE_STARS) — it does not raise the cost of any card mulched afterward; mulch cost is always the Dirt printed on the card being mulched.
  • Dice bag: all 5 colours return each round; exactly 4 dice actions happen per round, so 1 colour always goes undrawn.
  • The Oaks tab shows the real tower boards for reference; the small numbers there are just this prototype's simple 0–6 position tracker. All 6 rungs' printed rewards are now live for every Oak (fired on every climb, from any source): Red 3 +1 Bulb + Activate a Sprout, 4 +2 Bulb + a Sprout, 5 a Sprout + Activate a Sprout, 6 Activate 3 different Sprouts; Blue 3 Grow & activate a card (full cost), 4 +2 Water + 2 of any resource, 5 2 of any resource + 3 Points, 6 Grow & activate a card (free); Yellow 3 pay 1 Sun to re-activate any Oak's rung 4 (no Tag), 4 +2 Sun + 3 Points, 5 trade 1-5 Sun for double Points, 6 free re-activate 3 different Oaks' rung 4; Black 3 mulch a card at -2 Dirt (no activation), 4 +2 Dirt then mulch & activate (full), 5 activate 2 different mulched Bases, 6 mulch a card for free (no activation); White 3 discard a card for its Tag (max 1, wild picks any), 4 draw 2 then discard one to fully activate it, 5 discard for Tag + 3 Points, 6 draw 4 then discard 3 to activate them one at a time. The canopy treetop bonuses are now live too (see the Tags note below) — each colour has 5 printed options a player picks between when claiming: Black go up 2 different Oaks (no reward), activate a mulched Base +2 Points, mulch 2 cards (combined -2 Dirt, no activation), mulch 1 card +3 Points (no activation), climb any Oak (full reward); Blue any 5 resources (mix and match), collect from any wheel quadrant, grow at -2 discount Water-or-Sun (no activation), any 2 resources (mix and match) +3 Points, activate 2 different Sprouts; Red gain 6 Bulb, perform another action with the current die, a Sprout +2 Points, 5 Points, activate 2 different Sprouts; White draw 6 Cards, activate a mulched Base +2 Points, mulch at -1 Dirt +2 Points (no activation), discard a card to activate it +3 Points, any Tag (wild) +2 Points; Yellow activate any Oak at rung 4+, grow & activate a card (full cost), grow at a -2 discount pool Water-then-Sun (activates), activate 2 different Oaks at rung 2+, any Tag (wild) +2 Points. The rung-level purple stars each Oak feeds into final score are the same 2/3/4/5/6/8 track for all 5 — see the Scoring note above.
