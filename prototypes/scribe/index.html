Overview

The Pharaoh requires a new Royal Bureaucrat! Scribe hieroglyphs onto your sacred text using a flip and write selection: scribe the top of one card and perform the action at the bottom of the other.

Carefully balance your grid to fulfil objectives and positional scoring, while performing your unique ritual puzzle.

Key Features

  • Multiple conflicting scoring opportunities
  • Simultaneous play
  • Action manipulation and bonus actions
