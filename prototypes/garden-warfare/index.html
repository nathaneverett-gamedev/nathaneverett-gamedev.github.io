Garden Warfare

Overview

The garden is brimming for war. Two sides of the fence. Critters, flowers and fae alike want the ground. Can you gain dominance and win the turf.

Garden Warfare is a grid battler where you must place critters in a 3x3 grid to outscore the directly adjacent opponent critter to gain win tokens. The player with the most win tokens will win the game.

But watch out, critters will have a range of abilities and scoring powers to make use of and out-play your opponent.
