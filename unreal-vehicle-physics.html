Unreal Vehicle Physics

Overview

The project began as a test: could World of Tanks' vehicle physics simulation be recreated in Unreal Engine? That simulation became the foundation for Tomorrow Falls, a 15v15 hero shooter with vehicular combat, developed by DPS Games and published by Wargaming. The game was later cancelled.

I built and maintained automated tests that kept the simulation behaving correctly as it grew from a proof of concept into the basis of a full game.

What I Worked On

  • Wrote automated tests in Unreal Engine and Python to check the vehicle physics simulation's behaviour.
  • Ran the tests in continuous integration builds so regressions showed up early.
  • Kept the suite running as the simulation moved from a proof of concept into production on Tomorrow Falls.
