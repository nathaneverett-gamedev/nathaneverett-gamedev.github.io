Software Developer · Game Designer · Sydney

I'm Nathan Everett, a game developer who works across the client and the backend, with a background in design, tooling and QA. Most recently I built UI and backend features for Valorant at Riot Games. Before that I worked on BigWorld engine tooling and test automation at Wargaming, and on mobile games at Foundation Mobile.

I also design board games. My first, War of Supremacy, was funded on Kickstarter by more than 300 backers and shipped worldwide. You can play some of my current prototypes in your browser.
